## Honesty notes

- Labels are FAMILY-level: rule L1 (cat 5) fires on any "output = input plus
  added cells" transform, which spans rays, segment extension, connection,
  and growth — one label, many mechanisms. The labels rank op-investment;
  they do not promise any specific op solves any specific task. Conversion
  is measured (§4), never assumed.
- Cat 10 (445, 47%) is the honest residual: shape-changing, multi-mechanism,
  or compositional tasks the rules do not capture. Per protocol §3.1, no op
