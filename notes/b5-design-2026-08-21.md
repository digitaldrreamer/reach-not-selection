# B5 design — triangulated op-proposal (Amendment A3, pulled forward)

**Status: DESIGN NOTE.** No B5 run may happen until a separate prereg
manifest freezes the details marked TBD-PREREG below. Packaging-priority
guard (A3): B5 pauses if the first real Kaggle submission has not happened
by 2026-09-15.

## Question

Tiers 3–4 measured taxonomy mining running dry (+7, then +2 per ten ops)
and an exhaustive eval-zero at len ≤ 3. B5 asks the TPD-shaped question in
its licensed, extensional form: **does triangulated provenance select
better DSL ops than alternative selection from the same candidate pool at
matched op budget?**

## Arms

- **T (treatment):** ≤ 10 ops selected by two-media triangulation from a
  candidate universe U.
- **R (provenance control; […]):** 10 ops drawn at random from
  the same U (seeded, TBD-PREREG). This is the decisional pair — it
  isolates provenance from the value of U itself.
- **X (context arm, exploratory):** the realized taxonomy ops (tier 3's
  ten) as a same-budget external reference. T-vs-X is exploratory: the two
  arms use different information sources, so no provenance claim rides on
  it.

## Arm T pipeline (extensional only; […])

1. **Candidate universe U** (~30–60 deterministic Grid→Grid closures
   implemented up front, a superset of mechanisms NOT in v4: parameterized
   rotations/reflections about object axes, object sorting/ranking moves,
   mirror-about-axis, counting constructs, per-object scaling, color
   arithmetic, …). U is frozen in the prereg BEFORE any medium trains.
   No candidate may be derived from eval data or from per-task probe rows.
2. **Synthetic family generator:** task families whose ground-truth
   transforms are sampled compositions over U applied to random grids
   (re-ARC style, generator seeded). Families are the training substrate
   for both media; real ARC tasks are never used for discovery.
3. **Two media, independently trained on the same families:**
   - M1: symbolic enumerator with a redundant basis over U;
   - M2: small neural learner (~1M params, CPU-trainable, grid-pair
     in/out — reusing […] machinery).
4. **Discovery + triangulation:** per candidate op u, M1's evidence =
   load-bearing usage frequency in minimal solutions; M2's evidence =
   frozen correspondence procedure (adapted from […]) scoring
   whether M2 carries a unit/feature tracking u's applicability. The
   survivor floor = ops passing BOTH media's frozen bars (bars TBD-PREREG,
   set before discovery outputs are seen). Top ≤ 10 by a frozen rule → T.
5. **Injection + measurement:** T ops added to the DSL as a tier; §4
   metric on ARC-AGI-2 training (N_T, C_T, FP_T, regression guard) at the
   A1 budget; one eval read. Identically for R (and X is already
   measured).

## Pre-filed reading (bars TBD-PREREG before any outcome is seen)

- The decisional comparison is **T vs R at matched op count**: triangulated
  provenance should beat random provenance from the same U (the F2
  analog). A null here is a real, publishable negative for op-proposal-by-
  triangulation on this surface.
- T vs X is descriptive context either way.

## Fallback and kill conditions (honesty recorded now)

- If M2 cannot clear a frozen competence bar on held-out synthetic
  families by 2026-09-30, either (a) fall back to a second INDEPENDENT
  symbolic medium (different search basis) with the cross-medium claim
  explicitly weakened to cross-instrument, or (b) kill arm T and record
  the boundary. Choice is the researcher's, logged before proceeding.
- A3 guard: packaging milestone outranks B5 at every conflict.

## Effort estimate (honest)

U implementation + generator: ~1 week. M2 training loop on CPU at toy
scale: days (machinery exists). Correspondence adaptation: the risky
chunk, ~1–2 weeks. Total 2–4 part-time weeks, parallel-safe with
packaging except for CPU contention during measurement runs.
